## Overview

During my **AGIbot internship (June 2026 – present)**, I work on **humanoid object interaction**, including multiple humanoid robots collaboratively carrying a shared object. The work focuses on coordinated interaction with objects and real-robot deployment.

This ongoing work is inspired by [TeamHOI](https://github.com/sail-sg/TeamHOI). We are working toward transferring coordinated multi-humanoid carrying from simulation to real robots.

</div>

<div class="lang-zh" markdown="1">

## 简介

在 **AGIbot 智元机器人实习期间（2026 年 6 月至今）**，我从事**人形机器人与物体交互**研究，包括多个人形机器人协作搬运共享物体，重点关注机器人与物体的协调交互及实机部署。

这项正在进行的工作受到 [TeamHOI](https://github.com/sail-sg/TeamHOI) 启发，目前正推动多人形机器人协作搬运从仿真走向实机。

</div>
